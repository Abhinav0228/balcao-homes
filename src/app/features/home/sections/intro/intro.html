Who we are

Built from the hill behind them

Many of these houses stand empty, shared between a dozen heirs, losing a few tiles every monsoon. We take them on one at a time.

A Goan house of the 1800s came from the ground it stands on: red laterite cut from the hill, lime burnt from shells, clay tiles laid on teak. We settle the title, restore it with the same materials and the masons who still know how, and sell it, finished, to people who will live in it.
